About My Work

I'm a physicist specializing in gravitational-wave astrophysics, with a focus on gravitational lensing of these cosmic signals. I recently completed my Ph.D. at ICTS-TIFR under Prof. Parameswaran Ajith.

My research combines theoretical physics, computational modeling, and data analysis to explore how gravitational waves can reveal hidden aspects of the universe.
