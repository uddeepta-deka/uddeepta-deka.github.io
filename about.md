I’m Uddeepta. Thanks for visiting my corner of the web. I am an astrophysicist, currently working as a postdoctoral fellow at the [Shanghai Astronomical Observatory - Chinese Academy of Sciences (SHAO - CAS)](https://www.shao.ac.cn/). My work at the gravitational-wave (GW) and relativistic fundamental astronomy group at SHAO lies in the intersection of GW physics, computational modelling, and data analysis. I am particularly interested in gravitational lensing of GWs - how intervening compact objects and other structures can reshape these signals, and what those distortions can tell us about the lenses themselves.

I grew up in Doomdooma, a small and culturally vibrant town in Tinsukia, Assam. My journey through physics began at Ramjas College, [University of Delhi](https://www.du.ac.in/), followed by a master's degree in Physics from the [Department of Physics and Astrophysics](https://physics.du.ac.in/) at the same university. I later completed my PhD at the [International Centre for Theoretical Sciences](https://icts.res.in/), under the guidance of [Prof. Parameswaran Ajith](https://home.icts.res.in/~ajith/Home.html). 

I’m passionate about science outreach and love engaging with students of all ages. From organizing public talks and night sky observation sessions with ICTS’s in-house telescope to collaborating with the ICTS Outreach Team on activities for local schools, I strive to bridge the gap between academia and the broader community. Teaching and mentoring are close to my heart; I’ve guided younger students with term papers and projects in a number of graduate school courses, particularly on GW.

When I’m not doing research, you might find me on the badminton court, catching up with friends, trekking, taking a long drive, or getting lost in a good book. Occassionally, I play percussion instruments and the Indian bamboo flute -- finding rhythm and melody to be great outlets for creativity. Languages fascinate me too, and I enjoy learning new languages.

I try jot down my thoughts and musings on my [personal blog](https://megh-ud.blogspot.com/).

If you would like to know more, you can explore my research, or get in touch. I hope we can connect to share ideas, curiosity, and a passion for exploring the cosmos! Thanks for stopping by!
