## 🎓 Current Position & Research

I'm a physicist having recently completed my Ph.D. at the [International Centre for Theoretical Sciences (ICTS-TIFR)](https://icts.res.in/), under the guidance of [Prof. Parameswaran Ajith](https://home.icts.res.in/~ajith/Home.html). 

**My research** lies at the intersection of theoretical physics, computational modeling, and data analysis. I work on **gravitational-wave astrophysics**, particularly in the **gravitational lensing** of these signals.

---

## 🌏 Background & Journey

I hail from **Doomdooma**, a small yet culturally rich town in Tinsukia, Assam. My academic journey began with:

- **B.Sc. in Physics** — Ramjas College, University of Delhi
- **M.Sc. in Physics and Astrophysics** — University of Delhi  
- **Ph.D. in Physics** — ICTS-TIFR, Bangalore

I moved to ICTS to delve deeper into the fascinating world of gravitational-wave astrophysics, where I've spent the last several years pushing the boundaries of what we know about lensed gravitational signals.

## 🌟 Outreach & Teaching

I'm passionate about **science outreach** and love engaging with students of all ages. My outreach activities include:

- **Public talks & lectures** on gravitational waves and astrophysics
- **Night sky observation sessions** with ICTS's in-house telescope
- **Collaborating with ICTS Outreach Team** on activities for local schools
- **Mentoring students** — guided an M.Sc. student on her thesis
- **Teaching support** — helping students with term papers and projects in graduate courses on gravitational waves

I believe in bridging the gap between academia and the broader community, making science accessible and exciting for everyone.
